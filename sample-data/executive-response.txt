🏛️ BOARDROOM VERDICT

🎯 Opportunity
The idea addresses a genuine convenience problem, but it should begin with a tightly defined service area rather than a broad initial rollout.

🔍 Key Findings
- Working professionals and busy households are the clearest early customer segments.
- Reliability and turnaround time matter more than broad service variety.
- A lightweight launch channel can validate demand before a full platform build.

💰 Commercial View
Start with simple package pricing and validate costs with real local transport, labour and delivery assumptions.

⚠️ Major Risks
- inconsistent service quality
- failed or delayed pickups
- customer acquisition costs
- damage or lost items

🗺️ Execution Roadmap
1. Select one pilot area.
2. Interview 20 potential customers.
3. Sign two or three operating partners.
4. Run a four-week pilot.
5. Measure repeat usage, margins and complaints.

✅ Final Recommendation
VALIDATE FIRST.
